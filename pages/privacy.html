Privacy Policy

At Aniz, Inc., your privacy and trust are paramount. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website forms, we collect the following information:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Service of interest or inquiry subject
  • Preferred contact method
  • Any message or comments you provide

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or service request
  • Follow up on your behalf by phone, email, or text as you've requested
  • Connect you with the appropriate Aniz, Inc. service or program
  • Improve our services based on aggregate feedback (never linked to you individually)

We do NOT:

  • Sell your information to third parties
  • Share your information with outside organizations without your consent
  • Use your information for marketing purposes beyond responding to your specific request
  • Store protected health information (PHI) on this website

3. Protected Health Information (PHI) & HIPAA

Important: This website is not a HIPAA-compliant system and does not store Protected Health Information (PHI). Do not include:

  • Specific medical diagnoses or conditions
  • Medication names or dosages
  • Social Security numbers
  • Insurance information
  • Detailed health history

If you need to discuss sensitive health information, please call us directly at (404) 521-2410 or visit us in person at our office (236 Forsyth Street SW Suite #300, Atlanta, GA 30303).

HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices

Aniz, Inc. is a covered entity under HIPAA and maintains strict confidentiality standards for all protected health information in our care. Our full HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices is available at our office or upon request.

Call (404) 521-2410 to request a copy of our complete Notice of Privacy Practices or email contact@aniz.org.

4. Data Retention Policy

Website form submissions (name, email, phone, service interest) are reviewed by our team and securely deleted within 90 days unless:

  • You become an active client or participant in an Aniz, Inc. program (in which case information is transferred to our secure clinical systems)
  • You explicitly request to continue our relationship
  • We are required to retain information by law

5. Security Measures

We take your privacy seriously and employ industry-standard security practices:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Restricted access to form data (staff only)
  • Secure storage of submissions in Google Sheets with password-protected access
  • Regular review and deletion of inactive submissions

6. Newsletter Subscription (Optional)

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. We use it only to send you updates about Aniz, Inc. programs and events. We comply with CAN-SPAM regulations and provide an unsubscribe option in every email.

7. External Links

Our website may link to external sites (e.g., Microsoft Forms for the registration form). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites. Please review their privacy policies.

8. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics (anonymized) to understand how visitors interact with our site. This does not personally identify you. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking in your browser settings.

9. Contact for Privacy Questions

If you have questions about this privacy policy, how we handle your data, or to request deletion of your information, please contact us:

Aniz, Inc.
236 Forsyth Street SW Suite #300
Atlanta, GA 30303
(404) 521-2410
contact@aniz.org

10. Changes to This Policy

Aniz, Inc. may update this privacy policy as our practices evolve. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

Your privacy is a right, not a favor. We are committed to serving Atlanta communities with both clinical excellence and deep respect for your confidentiality.

Aniz, Inc. promotes emotional and physical wellness through mental health services, sexual health education, and holistic support for individuals affected by health disparities.
