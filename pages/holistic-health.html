Holistic Health Promotion

Aniz, Inc. is guided by Holistic Health Promotion best practices. We rely upon evidence-based approaches in treating addiction and activities that have a negative impact on health.

The Veil of Secrecy

In 2001, Zina Age wrote an article called "The Veil of Secrecy" in which she outlined her vision for Aniz's approach to intervention and treatment. The Aniz philosophy has developed over years of adapting and meeting the needs of every member who walks into the Aniz headquarters.

Our Philosophical Framework

Social Determinants of Health

Individuals are shaped by a wide range of external circumstances that are often beyond their personal control — including societal racism, learning disorders, geographic location, and genetic predisposition. Aniz recognizes that no individual can be held solely responsible for health outcomes influenced by these broader forces.

Smoke-Screen

Society often maintains hierarchies through systems of oppression, causing marginalized groups to absorb messages of inferiority. Even inherent characteristics become stigmatized through sustained systemic messaging. This internalization creates deep barriers to self-acceptance and well-being.

At Risk Populations

Vulnerable individuals who internalize stigmatizing messages from society can develop profound feelings of secrecy, shame, and guilt. These psychological burdens extend well beyond self-image and affect overall functioning, identity formation, and the ability to seek help.

The Face of Addiction

Certain populations lack adequate tools to counter negative societal messaging and build healthy emotional responses. Rather than processing difficult emotions constructively, some individuals turn to substance use or risky behaviors as an escape. These choices represent maladaptive coping strategies for deep, unresolved internal conflicts.

Removing the Veil

Initial substance use can create temporary relief from societal negativity — but this escape is illusory. Upon returning to reality, individuals face the original external pressures alongside the new burden of addiction. This dual weight complicates recovery and compounds the challenges already present.

Spectrum of Behaviors

Aniz's approach involves communicating in language that clients understand, and actively working to eliminate stigmatizing messaging from every interaction. This creates genuinely non-judgmental dialogue — a sharp contrast to society's historically negative framing — enabling real connection and meaningful therapeutic progress.

Holistic Harm Reduction Goals

Perfection is an unobtainable goal. Rather than strive for perfection, the Aniz model accepts the person "as is" and starts from the very basic position of total acceptance. Aniz prioritizes maximizing overall health and well-being over demanding abstinence as the sole measure of success.

Holistic Harm Reduction Strategies

Aniz recognizes addiction as another layer of societal stigma rather than a personal moral failure. The model begins from a foundation of total acceptance of each individual's current circumstances. The fundamental goal is health optimization and well-being — not abstinence alone.
